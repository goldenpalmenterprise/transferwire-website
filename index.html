Behind the numbers

How our values are made.

Every area is powered by its own AI agent – developed in-house, trained on football data and domain expertise, and continuously optimised. It processes performance data, market movements and reports at high complexity and delivers precise, traceable results.

  • Club seeksAI agent

    detects needs and calculates how likely a club really is to sign.

  • Player MarketAI agent

    connects offered players with matching club needs.

  • TransfersAI agent

    checks every report for signal strength and source trust.

  • PerformanceAI agent

    rates every match and every player on a scale from 0 to 100.

  • Market valueAI agent

    calculates the TW market value from performance, league and market – for every league.

The agents keep learning: results are reviewed continuously, rules are sharpened and features keep evolving.
